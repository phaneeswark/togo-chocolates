GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
to know.

TOGO is taking shape. Here is what the current bar and packaging concepts show.

Which chocolates are in the collection?+

Milk and dark chocolate bars with nuts, berries, or nuts and berries together. White chocolate is not part of this collection.

Are there full and half-size bars?+

Both sizes are represented in the packaging concepts. Final weights and availability will be shared when ordering opens.

Can I order online now?+

Online checkout is not live yet. Save your preferred bars in the enquiry bag and copy your selection for later.

Are the bars certified organic?+

Not yet. The packaging images are concepts, and no organic certification is being claimed.

THE PLACE BEHIND THE NAME ↗

THE NAME & THE PLACE

A landscape
worth savouring.

TO comes from తూర్పు (East), and GO from గోదావరి (Godavari). Together, TOGO carries East Godavari in its name.

Its visual world draws from Konaseema: coconut palms, river light, and the slow rhythm of the delta.
